    • 🔑 resets a forgotten PIN back to 1234. 🗑 removes someone — their past entries stay in every report.

    Permanent removal
    • 🗑 deletes a walk-in, phone entry or sale from the records for good. Totals, charts, the leaderboard and Excel reports update straight away.
    • Use it for entries that should never exist — test taps, duplicates, the wrong person. For a normal slip, the − button or Undo is enough.

    Publishing an update
    1. Change the app-version number near the top of the file (for example 2026.10.20-1).
    2. Upload the file in place of the current one. Keep sw.js in the same folder.
    3. That's all. Open devices refresh themselves within about 2 minutes, or straight away when the app is reopened. Nobody is logged out and nothing needs clearing or reinstalling.

    If you forget step 1, devices still notice the file changed and update a few minutes later. A refresh waits while someone has a form or this panel open.
